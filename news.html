📰 Sunbury Daily Chronicle — December 29, 1977

Guests Report “Unusual Activity” at Newly Reopened JackyWacky World

Sunbury, Victoria — Less than two weeks after Hypolistic Entertainment reopened JackyWacky World, several guests have reported unsettling experiences inside the park’s animatronic zones.

Witnesses describe endoskeleton performers “moving off‑script,” pausing mid‑routine, and in one case, turning their heads toward guests despite being powered down for maintenance.

Hypolistic Entertainment denies any malfunction, stating the incidents were “misinterpretations of normal animatronic behavior.”

However, one guest claims an endoskeleton in the Funhouse Corridor whispered something as she walked past.

“It said… ‘We remember.’ I know what I heard.”

Park officials have refused to release maintenance logs from the reopening week.

— Sunbury Daily Chronicle, 1977
